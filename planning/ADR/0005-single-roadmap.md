# ADR-0005 — todo.md retired; ROADMAP.md is the single roadmap

- **Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-07-08

## Context
`todo.md` claimed to be "kept in sync" and was provably stale (listed shipped
v0.3.0 work as in-progress).

## Decision
Deleted; its live items were absorbed into `planning/ROADMAP.md`'s phases and
backlog. There is exactly one roadmap document.
